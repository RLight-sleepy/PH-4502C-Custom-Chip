# PH-4502C-Custom-Chip
A Custom Chip for the Sensor 'PH-4502C' in Wokwi!

***

Wokwi is genuinely a good site, and it's one of the few good sites because it has this one great feature—**Custom Chips!**  
I don't have money for Proteus, and I really wouldn't have the need to make something like this if I did have Proteus. But, here we are lol.

I created this and two other chips because of a term paper I'm doing and Wokwi didn't have the chips I needed :(( So I made it myself!
These two files `ph-4502c.chip.c` and `ph-4502c.chip.json` are free of use! I don't really care if you credit me or not, I'm not using my real name anyways!
Enjoy!

## Installation

### OPTION 1: Download and upload the files

1. Download both `ph-4502c.chip.c` and `ph-4502c.chip.json`. They're virus free, I swear.
   
2. Go to your Wokwi project, click the dropdown arrow at the very end of the tabs
<img width="1081" height="69" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/04faf6e4-b43b-4fcf-b3df-456e008a193c" />

3. Click `Upload file(s)...`

4. Choose both `ph-4502c.chip.c` and `ph-4502c.chip.json` in your file manager, and click **'open'** or **'upload'** depending on your file manager.

5. There you have it!
